#Class_α
**DM only.** The real story behind [[AA-Roann]] and the public [[Timeline]]. Decided 2026-10 with Claude; lines marked *(suggestion)* are Claude's glue between your decisions, so change them freely.

## In short
The gods never got bored. **Corruption**, a greedy god who wanted Roann all to itself, sealed the [[The Korion|Korion]] around 2535 [[Golden Age|b.g.a.]] and locked the other gods out. Only one other god was caught inside: **the god of magic**. In year 0 the two fought. Corruption won, but the fight crashed into the world as "the falling star" at [[THE Rift]]. The god of magic died and its power scattered as wild mana, which is why magic suddenly spread to everyone: the Magic Golden Age. Corruption, injured, sank into the void at the bottom of the [[Deep hole]] (connected underground to the Rift). It has been healing ever since.

## The real timeline
- **~20k b.g.a.** The god of magic makes a pact with certain families, mostly nobles (the "first contact with ancient species" in the [[Archives]]). That's why only nobles had magic before year 0.
- **~2535 b.g.a.** Corruption places the seal around the Korion to keep the other gods out. Researchers near [[The First Library]] notice the sky beyond the galaxy go empty, and the [[Trapped in the unknown]] hypothesis is born. The god of magic is trapped inside with Corruption.
- **200 b.g.a. – 0** Magic slowly starts spreading beyond the nobles. *(suggestion: this growing power is what made greedy Corruption attack)*
- **Year 0** Corruption and the god of magic fight. It gets out of hand; the impact opens [[THE Rift]], which connects underground to the void of the Deep Hole's 13th layer. Corruption wins but is badly injured and sinks into the void. The god of magic dies, its power scatters as wild mana, and magic floods to everyone: the Magic Golden Age begins.
- **Year 0** [[Miric Abdul]], a 7-year-old prodigy, creates the [[Miric Calendar]] and names its months, days and moons after the forgotten gods. *How did he know?* #todo
- **0 – 400** Faith fades. The schools teach that the gods got bored and left.
- **400 – 500** The concepts start to fray: stillbirths (Life failing), ghosts that can't leave (Death failing), faces and whole cities losing their existence, people drawn to drown or jump.
- **663** Anton Viln blames wild mana. He's half right: the dead god's mana has settled into the world and patches the cracks for a while, so the phenomena fade.
- **~1000** Corruption has healed enough to seep upward, and the phenomena return, milder. Its first open servants, a brute family, overpower the others and found the [[Gunlags Dominion]] ("Strength and will above all").
- **1295 (now)** Corruption feeds on everything that goes down the Deep Hole (delvers, mana, rare materials) and seeps up through the layers. Monsters gather where mana is concentrated.

## Who knows what
- **The Watchers** (Class Z) know everything. They are the gods' eyes, cut off from their masters, still appearing at moments that could change Roann.
- **[[Valyriss]]** is a Watcher in disguise, living at [[Elemental city of Echath|Echath]] as the Keeper of Secrets of The First Library. She knows the most of anyone in Roann.
- **The 12 Headmasters** keep a pact of silence (Class G), which is why every textbook says the gods got bored.
- **Corruption's servants** (the Gunlags ruling family, maybe others) know their part and guard the lie.
- **The public** knows the "bored gods" story and that "the falling star brought magic". It's close to the truth, but they think it was a rock.

## If the seal breaks
The gods come back to help. They can mend Life and Death and fight Corruption, which makes breaking the seal the good ending, if anyone survives getting to it.

## Clues already in the vault
- [[Trapped in the unknown]]: the sphere is the seal.
- [[The Watchers]]: horned, silent, and present at world-changing events. [[Valyriss]] has the same horns.
- [[Deep hole]]: the 13th layer "void", and 87 returnees. *(suggestion: accounts from before year 0 describe a real bottom; later ones describe the void)*
- [[Lost city]]: "uncontrollable, non-absorbable mana" left after seven families vanished, which is the dead god's remains.
- [[Miric Calendar]]: the gods' names, hidden in plain sight.
