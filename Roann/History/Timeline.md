#Class_A
Public timeline of [[AA-Roann]], in [[Miric Calendar]] years (b.g.a. / [[Golden Age|a.g.a.]]). Years with ~ are approximate. Player-safe: this is what scholars in 1295 would tell you.

## Before the Golden Age
- **~740k b.g.a.** First signs of intelligent human life.
- **~100k – 80k b.g.a.** First records, found in caves.
- **~20k b.g.a.** The [[Archives]] begin. They tell of first contact with ancient species and a pact with certain families, mostly nobles. From then on, magic belongs to the nobility.
- **~2535 b.g.a.** Near [[The First Library]], researchers first suggest that Roann is [[Trapped in the unknown|trapped in a sphere]].
- **~1700 b.g.a.** The [[Deep hole]] has been called "the hole" since about now.
- **~200 b.g.a.** The Great Union Age: the first trade between kingdoms and territories.
- **200 b.g.a. – 0** The kingdoms settle down, and magic slowly starts reaching small villages.

## The Golden Age (0 – 400)
- **0** A falling star strikes the far west and opens [[THE Rift]]. People say it brought magic: the same year, magic floods the world and the Magic Golden Age begins. [[Miric Abdul]], only 7, creates the new calendar.
- **~16** Miric Abdul dies, at about 23.
- **~20** The education boom: [[Iremorth Kingdom]], [[Tynead Kingdom]] and [[Kaspion Empire]] open schools and sell books to commoners.
- **28** Masahabi astrologists name the universe [[The Korion]] and settle on [[Alerog]] and [[Anopo]] as the farthest moons.
- **0 – 400** Faith fades. The gods are replaced by war heroes or forgotten. The schools teach that the gods simply got bored and left.
- **279** [[Oliruf]] is first spotted.

## The Strange Years (400 – 663)
- **400 – 500** Strange things begin all over Roann: more monsters, people disappearing, whole cities gone overnight, ghosts, stillbirths, faces losing their features, sounds from nowhere, people drowning themselves or jumping from heights.
- **663** [[Anton Viln]] of [[Batun Tower]] finishes his research: wild mana is to blame (see the [[Lost city]]). It explains the small cases, not the big ones. Over the next years, the phenomena fade.

## The Quiet Centuries (663 – 1000)
- **~700** The [[Bureau of Central Kaspion]] is founded. Delving the Deep Hole becomes official: permits, safehouses and ranked [[Dwelling-Delving gears]].
- **~840** "On the future of the Skigness peninsula" is written.
- **~860** Its author dies, and the book rises to fame.
- **~870** The Skigness city-states unite as the [[Collio Republic]].

## The Return (1000 – 1295)
- **~1000** The phenomena return, milder than before. One brute family overpowers the others and founds the [[Gunlags Dominion]].
- **~1000 – 1100** The Gunlags wars: the new Dominion fights its neighbours.
- **~1050** The [[Shiva Islands]] go dark. Vampires take [[The tower of Rekku]], and the villages beyond the [[Shiva peaks]] freeze in time.
- **~1150 onward** The west of the [[Ardess Mist Realm]] slips out of control: hags around [[Aryss town]] and the lake, and the [[Rhesa Woodland]].
- **~1200** Cartels take hold between [[Dor]] and [[Batun Tower]]: drugs and a black market in people.
- **1273** [[Ricia Tyne]] is born.
- **1279** The fire. Ricia, 6, is badly burned and found by the [[Tyne]] family, who adopt her.
- **1295** Now.
