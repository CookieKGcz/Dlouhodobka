In the Newtonian theory, the gravity given by a certain mass M at distance R is given by 𝑔=GM/R2
Gravity: ~1 g (Earth-like). With r = 3824km that needs a very dense planet, ~9.2 g/cm³ (Earth is 5.5), so Roann is a mostly-metal world. That's why ores like [[Mitrite]] are common. Mass ≈ 2.15 × 10^24 kg (about 0.36 Earth masses).

Map stretch: the map is a flat 2:1 picture of the globe, so east–west distances get stretched the further you are from the equator. Multiply east–west distances by cos(latitude): ×~0.75 around Tynead Castle and Batun (~40–45°N), ×~0.4 around Tyne Garden and northern Haford (~65°N+). North–south distances are right everywhere. On the 4000×2000 map: latitude = 90° − (pixel y ÷ 2000 × 180°).
Ages: 1 Roann year ≈ 2.7 Earth years, but magic stretches lives about as much, so ages in Roann years feel normal (humans: adult ~18, old ~80).
